- Requirement: define one complete bounded secret-free receipt for an
  immutable provider route before any product surface renders or persists it.
- Evidence and expected failure: the new stable-vector fixture first failed
  because route snapshots exposed separate identity/model/auth/policy getters
  but no inseparable serializable receipt. No production surface changed while
  the value contract was established.
- In scope: receipt revision, complete identity, optional bounded model,
  auth-source class, exact lossless time budgets, retry counts, catalog policy,
  bound-generation retry, current-generation semantic replay, disabled
  fallback, snapshot/current/inference/catalog projection, and fail-closed
  decoding. Out of scope: JSONL/schema, `/status`, doctor rendering, provider
  account/quota/billing, endpoints, headers, credentials, bodies, and dynamic
  provider data.
- Authority/failure/version/rollback notes: only an already validated immutable
  snapshot can produce a receipt. Runtime objects have no receipt field and
  cannot serialize. Unknown fields/variants and future revisions fail closed;
  zero/invalid durations, oversized models, and catalog contradictions are
  rejected. Source revert removes the unused projection without changing a
  provider send or persisted product schema.
- Tests added or changed: four provider integration fixtures pin a stable exact
  v1 vector, round-trip/getters, secret-marker absence despite a secret-bearing
  runtime, future/unknown/zero/invalid/oversized/catalog-mismatch rejection,
  catalog-only model absence, and exact generation one versus successor two
  receipts across a current-owner replacement.
- Implementation: `ProviderRouteReceipt` nests the existing validated route
  identity with a `ProviderRoutePolicyReceipt`. Exact durations serialize as
  seconds plus subsecond nanoseconds; closed execution-policy enums make retry,
  replay, and disabled fallback explicit. Every receipt-bearing owner projects
  from its immutable snapshot.

  deserialization self-validating even when used outside the enclosing receipt,
  added invalid-nanosecond and oversized-model cuts, and proved a stale bound
  receipt cannot drift to the current successor generation.
