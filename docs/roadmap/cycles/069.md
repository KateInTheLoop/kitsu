  physical HEAD verification alone did not decode or close canonical artifacts.

  logical/physical schema and identity/parent binding; one shared exact artifact

  artifact failures; true-missing, valid artifact-free, valid closed-artifact,
  malformed logical, identity disagreement, missing artifact, digest tamper, and

  selected corrupt HEAD remains selected for diagnosis; rollback requires a
  later explicit verified recovery operation. Current v1 remains production.
