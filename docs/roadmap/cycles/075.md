- Epic/story/card: E03 / E03-S04 / E03-S04-C3
- Requirement: define the complete provider-free value needed before recovery
  may publish. An available plan must retain every skipped corrupt revision and
  reason in deterministic selected-to-source order. An unavailable plan must
  retain the same verified prefix and identify the exact blocker. Recovery must
  assemble a new canonical revision from only a fully verified source; it must
  preserve the corrupt selected revision as its causal parent and never move
  HEAD backward.
- Evidence and expected failure: the requirements-first canonical fixtures did
  not compile because no recovery skip value, recovery ledger, or recovery
  assembler existed. Existing planner variants carried only selected state and
  recovery target, so they could not prove which revisions were bypassed or why
  traversal stopped. Adding the restart-authoritative ledger also made the old
  logical-schema-1 vectors fail closed until the explicit schema revision was
  advanced and all current/future fixtures were separated.
- In scope: canonical logical schema revision 2; a closed, bounded recovery
  ledger; pure recovery revision assembly; ordered corrupt skip evidence;
  precise unavailable blockers for selected state, root exhaustion, missing or
  mismatched parent-manifest digest, ancestor state, unsupported logical manifest,
  cycle, and traversal bound; schema-2/future-schema fixture updates. Out of
  scope: recovery writes, candidate installation, HEAD CAS, receipts,
  quarantine/GC, production mounting, branch/fork/export, alternate stores, and
  X005 promotion.
- Authority/failure/version/rollback notes: recovery planning and assembly are
  pure and grant no authority. Logical schema revision 2 is deliberate because
  recovery provenance changes restart-authoritative meaning; it is not hidden
  as an optional logical-v1 field. Existing production current-v1 files and
  paths remain untouched and authoritative, so reverting this private unmounted
  implementation remains exact rollback. Unknown logical schemas fail closed.
- Tests added or changed:

  - assembly rejects empty/wrong-first evidence, skipped source identity,

  - unavailable root, future selected, unverified selected manifest, and future

- Implementation: add `CanonicalRecoverySkipV1` and a closed v1 recovery binding
  to the canonical payload. Validation bounds and orders the ledger, rejects
  duplicate/ambiguous identities, validates all timestamps and source HEADs,
  and binds the current recovery entry to its revision/parent/HEAD/time. The
  pure assembler clones the verified source state, creates a distinct revision,
  sets its exact parent to the corrupt selected revision, clears compaction, and
  appends complete recovery provenance. The planner accumulates typed corrupt
  states while crossing only descriptor-pinned digest-bound envelopes and emits
  a typed blocker at every unavailable exit.
